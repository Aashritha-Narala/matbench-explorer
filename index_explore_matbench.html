What is Matbench?

Matbench is a standardized benchmarking suite for machine learning (ML) models in materials science. It provides a carefully curated set of diverse tasks to evaluate algorithmic performance.

Why do standardized benchmarks matter? In materials informatics, different researchers often use slightly different datasets or evaluation splits, making it nearly impossible to fairly compare models. Standardized benchmarks solve this by providing universal, fixed datasets and metrics. This allows the community to objectively identify the best-performing models, track historical progress, and ensure scientific reproducibility.
